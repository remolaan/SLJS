type: constitution
title: Constitution of Sri Lanka

Article 12: All persons are equal before the law and are entitled to the
equal protection of the law. No citizen shall be discriminated against on
the grounds of race, religion, language, caste, sex, political opinion,
place of birth or any one of such grounds.

Article 13: No person shall be arrested except according to procedure
established by law. Every person charged with an offence has the right to
be released on bail, subject to such conditions as may be prescribed by
law, unless there are reasonable grounds for believing that he or she will
not appear in accordance with such conditions. No person shall be deprived
of life, liberty or security of person except according to procedure
established by law.

Article 14: Every citizen is entitled to the freedom of speech and
expression, peaceful assembly, and freedom of movement.
