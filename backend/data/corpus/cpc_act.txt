type: statute
title: Code of Criminal Procedure Act, No. 15 of 1979

s.2: Criminal courts. There shall be the following classes of criminal
courts in Sri Lanka: the High Court, the Magistrate's Courts and such other
courts as may be established by law.

s.167: Trial before High Court. Trials before the High Court shall be by a
single judge and, save as otherwise provided, in accordance with the
provisions of this Code.

s.303: Framing of charges. The accused shall be informed of the charge
against him at the commencement of the trial and shall be asked whether he
pleads guilty or not guilty.
